How the numbers are calculated

  • Price rule: sales under $10,000 are left out of every figure on this page (40 of 35,442 rows), because they are not plausible home prices. All other sales are kept, including very high prices; the page reports medians, which outliers barely move.
  • Price per square foot uses only sales with a living area above 0 sq ft, so rows with a 0 or blank area do not divide by zero.
  • Days on market is contract_date minus list_date, counted only on sales that have both dates. A blank date is not treated as zero. Sales with no list date (often new construction or off-market sales) are left out of this figure, so it describes listed homes only. The "Has list date" column shows what share of each city's sales had one.
  • Every row is a separate sale. Rows are keyed by listing_id and never combined by street address, since many State College condo units share one address.
  • home_type is not used, because the data description sheet flags it as unreliable.
  • Campus area is the dataset's university column: the nearest campus to each home's coordinates.

Data: College-town home sales dataset, version 1.0 (2026). University of Illinois-Deloitte Foundation Center for Business Emerging Technologies. Data collected March 2026.
